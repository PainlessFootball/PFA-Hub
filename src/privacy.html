Privacy Policy

Effective: August 2026 · Draft, not yet legally reviewed
This is a starting draft prepared for a private, non-commercial fantasy football community site. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before this page is relied on for real users, it should be reviewed by qualified counsel, particularly given the site's Massachusetts base of operations.

1. What We Collect

When you register for PFA Hub, we collect your email address, a display name you choose, and — if you set one — a profile photo. We also store ordinary usage data such as chat messages, news posts, and applications you submit through the Site.

2. How We Use It

Collected information is used to operate your account, display your activity within the Site (chat, news, standings, directory), and communicate with you about your account (verification, password resets, approval status).

3. Firebase / Google

PFA Hub is built on Firebase, a platform operated by Google LLC. Account authentication, your profile data, and uploaded content are processed and stored through Firebase's services. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google itself handles data on Firebase's infrastructure.

4. Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from Settings, or by contacting an administrator.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. Profile fields (display name, avatar) can be edited directly in Settings; other requests can be sent to the contact address below.

6. Children

PFA Hub is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors, and no data covered by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) is knowingly collected.

7. Security

Account security is provided through Firebase Authentication. Optional two-factor authentication is available from Settings for additional account protection.

8. Contact

Data requests or privacy questions can be sent to painlessfootball@gmail.com.

9. Effective Date

This policy is effective as of August 2026 and may be updated as the Site evolves.
