Acceptable Use Policy

Effective: August 2026 · Draft, not yet legally reviewed
This is a starting draft prepared for a private, non-commercial fantasy football community site. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before this page is relied on for real users, it should be reviewed by qualified counsel, particularly given the site's Massachusetts base of operations.

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to all members of PFA Hub, including chat, news comments, and applications submitted through the Site.

1. Prohibited Content

  • Hate speech or discriminatory content directed at any individual or group
  • Harassment, threats, or targeted abuse of another member
  • Illegal content of any kind
  • Doxxing — sharing another person's private information without consent

2. Account Integrity

Fake accounts and impersonation of other members, administrators, or real people are not permitted. One account per person.

3. Fantasy Context

PFA Hub is a space for football and fantasy football discussion. Keep chat and news content within that context.

4. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the Site, at an administrator's discretion.

5. Reporting

If you see content or behavior that violates this policy, report it to an administrator directly or by email.

6. Contact

Reports and questions can be sent to painlessfootball@gmail.com.
